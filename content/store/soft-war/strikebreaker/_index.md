## The story so far...

{{< n-p >}}
The year is 3924, and crony capitalism has spread to the farthest reaches of the universe.
{{< /n-p >}}

{{< n-p >}}
A fuel refinery on the frozen rimworld Karthos-23b is embroiled in a full-blown labor strike. The workers are holding firm, but the security team has just arrested the two leaders of the fledgling union. Not only that, but they've called in a scab to keep the facility running.
{{< /n-p >}}

{{< n-p >}}
What nobody knows is that scab is secretly Dice Hagar, a hyper-lethal salt agent who's ready to agitate, and push the strike even further to beat the bosses.
{{< /n-p >}}

{{< n-p >}}
Things are gonna get a whole lot worse before they get better, and he's counting on that.
{{< /n-p >}}

{{< n-p >}}
Follow Dice's adventure as he fans the flames of labor revolution, and works from the shadows to overthrow the fascist mega-corp that owns the food-supply and the planet.
{{< /n-p >}}
